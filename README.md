# 🎬 My Top 3 Favorite Movies

## 1. World War Z (2013) 🧟

<img width="1280" height="1920" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ed47e59a-647e-4b72-82aa-5a030d9285b0" />

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Horror, Sci-Fi, Thriller

**Director:** Marc Forster

**Main Actor:** Brad Pitt

**Story:**
World War Z is a movie about a mysterious infection that spreads around the world and turns people into aggressive zombies. Gerry Lane, a former United Nations investigator, travels to different countries to find the source of the outbreak and discover a possible way to stop it.

**Why I Like This Movie:**
I like World War Z because it has exciting action scenes, suspenseful moments, and an interesting zombie storyline. It also shows how people work together and make difficult decisions during a worldwide crisis. The movie keeps me curious about what will happen next.

**Favorite Elements:**
- Exciting zombie scenes.
- Worldwide survival situations.
- Interesting storyline.
- Action and suspense.

## 2. Locked Up (2017) 🔒

<img width="480" height="640" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/78928560-4c21-4975-b280-2d6088412d89" />

**Genre:** Thriller, Drama

**Director:** Jared Cohn 

**Main Actress:** Kelly McCart

**Story:**
Locked Up is a prison-themed movie that focuses on the struggles and challenges experienced by people inside a prison. It explores difficult situations, personal conflicts, and the importance of survival in a challenging environment.

**Why I Like This Movie:**
I like this movie because of its intense atmosphere and interesting story. It shows how difficult situations can affect people's decisions and relationships. I also enjoy movies that have unexpected events and keep the audience wondering about what will happen next.

**Favorite Elements:**
- Suspenseful scenes.
- Interesting characters.
- Unexpected situations.
- Drama and tension.
---
## 3. The Green Inferno (2013) 🌿

<img width="1920" height="2560" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b6d8e6be-5f1a-4ecc-9609-d4ef742e8ec2" />

**Genre:** Horror, Adventure, Thriller

**Director:** Eli Roth

**Main Actress:** Lorenza Izzo

**Story:**
The Green Inferno follows a group of college students who travel to the Amazon rainforest to participate in an environmental protest. However, their journey takes a frightening turn after an unexpected accident leaves them stranded in an unfamiliar place. They must face difficult challenges as they try to survive.

**Why I Like This Movie:**
I like The Green Inferno because it has a unique survival horror story. The jungle setting makes the movie more mysterious and suspenseful. It also shows how people react when they face unexpected and dangerous situations.

**Favorite Elements:**
- Jungle survival theme.
- Suspenseful storyline.
- Unexpected twists.
- Horror and adventure.
